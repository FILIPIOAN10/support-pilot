# Shipping Policy

## Delivery times

Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 working days within the EU. Express delivery takes 1 to 2 working days and is available for orders placed before 14:00. Orders to islands and remote regions can take up to 3 extra days.

## Shipping costs

Standard delivery costs 4.90 EUR and is free for orders above 50 EUR. Express delivery costs 9.90 EUR regardless of the order value.

## Tracking

A tracking number is sent by email when the parcel leaves our warehouse. The tracking page updates every few hours. If the status does not change for 5 working days, contact support and we will open an investigation with the carrier.

## Lost parcels

A parcel is considered lost 15 working days after it was shipped. We then send a replacement or refund the order in full, at the choice of the customer.
