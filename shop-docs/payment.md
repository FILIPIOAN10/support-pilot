# Payment

## Accepted methods

We accept credit and debit cards, bank transfer and PayPal. Orders paid by bank transfer are shipped after the payment arrives, usually within 2 working days.

## Card security

Card payments use 3-D Secure authentication. We never store full card numbers; payments are processed by our payment provider.

## Failed payments

If a card payment fails, no money is taken. A pending authorisation disappears from the statement within 7 days. The customer can retry the payment from the order page for 24 hours, after which the order is cancelled automatically.

## Invoices

An invoice is emailed when the order ships. Companies can add a VAT number at checkout; invoices cannot be changed after they are issued.
