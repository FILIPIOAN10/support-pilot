# Returns Policy

## Return window

Items can be returned within 30 days of delivery. The item must be unused, in its original packaging and accompanied by the order number. The return window is counted from the delivery date shown in the tracking, not from the order date.

## How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are paid to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days after the returned item reaches our warehouse. Shipping costs of the original order are refunded only if the item was defective or the wrong item was sent.

## Items that cannot be returned

Hygiene products that have been opened, personalised items made to order, and digital gift cards cannot be returned. Opened software and sealed audio products with a broken seal are also excluded.

## Exchanges

Exchanges for another size or colour are free of charge once during the return window. If the new item costs more, the customer pays the difference; if it costs less, the difference is refunded.
